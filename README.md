# AI Blogging

Shared space for planning, discussing, and managing AI content for artificialintelligence-tech.com

## What we do here
- Collect AI topic ideas
- Outline blog posts and tutorials  
- Track writing progress
- Coordinate contributors

## How to contribute
1. Open a **New Issue** using our blog post template
2. Add the issue to our **Projects board** 
3. Move your card through: Backlog → Drafting → Review → Published

## Categories we cover
- AI News & Updates
- AI Tools & Tutorials  
- AI for Business/Productivity
- AI Coding & Data Science
- AI Ethics/Society
